About the Health Centre

The Student Health Care Centre at Sacred Heart College (Autonomous), Tirupattur provides comprehensive healthcare services for students and staff. The centre is equipped to handle first aid, general health consultations, medicine distribution, emergency care, and health awareness programs.

Mission

To provide quality healthcare services that promote physical, mental, and emotional well-being through compassionate care, timely medical assistance, and modern healthcare management.

Vision

To create a healthy campus by ensuring accessible, efficient, and technology-driven healthcare services for every student.

Our Objectives

First Aid

Immediate medical assistance for students.

Digital Records

Maintain secure electronic medical records.

Provide medicines safely and efficiently.

Health Awareness

Promote healthy lifestyles among students.
